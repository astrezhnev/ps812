Schedule

A schedule of topics and readings is provided below. Each week covers a single topic or group of topics. Tuesday lectures will typically introduce the core theory for the week while Wednesday lectures will go into greater detail and work through examples. You should make sure to review the readings prior to that week’s lectures with an aim towards completing them before Wednesday’s lecture.

The readings are not long, but they are dense. The first part of the semester in particular leans heavily on mathematical notation. Becoming comfortable with that kind of language is itself one of the learning goals of the course, so it will be tempting to skim past the math that looks daunting – do not do this. Read carefully, go line by line, take notes, and bring your questions to lecture, section, or the discussion board.

Textbook readings marked (AM) are from Aronow and Miller, Foundations of Agnostic Statistics, and those marked (AP) are from Angrist and Pischke, Mostly Harmless Econometrics. Both are available through the UW-Madison library; relevant excerpts will also be posted on Canvas. All hyperlinks to papers and to other books are either to the published version or to a freely available copy.

Readings:
